How it works

1

Readiness review

A fixed-scope review of how client ID and personal information moves through your firm. Carried out beside you, on your own screen, so we never need access to your systems.

2

Practical clean-up

We help you close the gaps that carry real risk, starting with what you’re holding and shouldn’t be. Every removal is logged with a path, a date and an authorising person.

3

Cyber Posture Statement

A dated, one-page statement of what was checked, what was fixed, and where you stand. Written to be read by your insurer months later without us in the room.

Less held, less to lose. Once ID copies have served their verification purpose, full copies aren’t needed for AML/CTF record-keeping. Destroying or de-identifying what you no longer need is the simplest risk reduction there is, and we help you prove it happened.
